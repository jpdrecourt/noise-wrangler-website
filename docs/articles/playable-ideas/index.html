Playable Ideas

Small toys for trying out musical ideas by hand. Each toy explores one idea. Its spec is written for AI agents: enough to rebuild the toy from scratch, in any language or on any hardware.

Play at play.noisewrangler.art

The toys are built for a large screen. On a phone, they offer a preview.

Motif and memory

Creating musical motifs out of random events.

  • motif-memory: Memorise the last two bars, replay them, and hear how far you can degrade them.
  • markov: Chain events by probability, with one step of memory.
  • statistical-feedback: Dice with memories.
  • branches: Grow motifs as branches, then weigh them.

SMC 2026, Zagreb

Four prototypes on memory, from my talk and poster at SMC 2026 in Zagreb on 7 November. The paper: Programming a music performance instrument for a wandering mind and clumsy hands.

These toys are part of a bigger project, exploring instrument design in conversation with other musicians. My design principle: humans are both fuzzy and fussy. Imprecise in what they ask or do, hard to please in what they accept.

Next could be strategies for field recordings, virtualisation, or whatever you are interested in. Tell me.
